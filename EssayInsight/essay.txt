Ever since I was young, I have always been passionate about helping others.
I am a person who believes in making a difference. I feel that my experiences
have made me who I am today. When I was in high school, I started a tutoring
program for underprivileged students in my community.

I think that education is the key to success. I organized weekly sessions
where I taught math and science to middle schoolers. I was nervous at first,
but I quickly learned to connect with the students. I believe this experience
taught me the importance of patience and communication.

I am excited to continue this work in college. I know that I want to study
education and eventually become a teacher. I feel that my background has
prepared me well for this path. At the end of the day, I just want to help
others succeed the way my mentors helped me.
